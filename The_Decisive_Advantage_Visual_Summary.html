01 — The Core Problem
The Activation Gap

Enterprises have spent years and billions building customer intelligence infrastructure. That work is largely done. Yet most cannot determine what to say to a customer in the next 200 milliseconds at the point of decision. This is not a data problem. It is a decisioning problem.

02 — The Business Drivers
Two Pressures. One Requirement.

Expansion Pressure

Grow revenue, acquire customers precisely, increase share of wallet. Individual-level decisions — not segments — drive 10–30% improvements in marketing ROI.

10–30% marketing ROI lift at the individual level

Defensive Pressure

Reduce cost, protect margin. At $11–12 per inbound contact, every call deflected to proactive digital resolution is direct savings. The decisioning loop must be genuinely proactive — not merely fast-reactive.

$11–12 per inbound service contact
03 — What's Broken
The Architecture That Broke

Legacy platforms maintain their own copy of customer data, separate from where intelligence lives. This pattern has become a liability.

⏱

Data Latency

Syncing every 4 hours cannot power real-time decisions. A customer who calls 3 hours after abandoning checkout needs a decision based on what just happened.

⚡

Integration Fragility

Every sync contract breaks. Schema changes, pipeline failures, identity mismatches — failures surface as wrong decisions at scale.

⚙

No AI Arbitration Path

Legacy rules engines cannot handle multiple models producing continuous scores — propensity, churn risk, LTV — evaluated simultaneously with contextual relevance.

04 — The Inflection Point
What Changed

Two years ago, this required a complex external stack. That world has compressed materially.
